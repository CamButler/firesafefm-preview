Services we provide

Commercial cleaning, done to a compliance standard

Six core services covering everything from a single deep clean to an ongoing, fully-documented maintenance contract.

01

Kitchen Deep Cleaning

Full professional deep clean of all kitchen surfaces, equipment, floors and hard-to-reach areas — ensuring hygiene compliance and a spotless result every time.

  • Full surface, equipment and floor degreasing
  • Hard-to-reach and behind-equipment cleaning
  • Hygiene-compliant finish, ready for inspection
02

Extraction Cleaning

Complete degreasing of canopy hoods, filters and extraction systems to TR19 standards — reducing fire risk and keeping your kitchen compliant with insurance requirements.

  • Canopy, hood, filter and ductwork degreasing
  • TR19 (DW/172) compliant methodology
  • Post-clean certification for insurers & inspectors
03

Ventilation Cleaning

Full internal ductwork and ventilation system cleaning — improving air quality, system efficiency and ensuring your facility meets regulatory standards.

  • Internal ductwork inspection and cleaning
  • Improved airflow and system efficiency
  • Supports HSE and building regulation compliance
04

Post-Renovation & Refurbishment Cleaning

After significant construction or renovation work, we provide a thorough clean to prepare your venue for full operational use — removing dust, debris and residue.

  • Full dust, debris and residue removal
  • Fixtures, fittings and surface clean-down
  • Site ready for handover or reopening
05

Sanitation & Hygiene-Focused Cleaning

High-standard sanitation services for environments requiring strict hygiene standards — ideal for healthcare, food and hospitality settings.

  • Hospital-grade sanitising products & methods
  • Tailored to healthcare and food-safety settings
  • Documented for audit and inspection purposes
06

Ongoing Maintenance Cleaning

Regular, scheduled cleaning contracts tailored to your facility's needs — keeping your premises clean, safe and well maintained at all times.

  • Weekly, monthly or quarterly scheduling
  • Consistent teams who know your site
  • Reporting after every visit

Why it matters

Compliance-focused. Detail-driven. Reliable.

TR19 (DW/172) is the industry standard for the cleanliness of kitchen extraction systems in the UK. Non-compliant ductwork is a leading cause of commercial kitchen fires and a common reason insurance claims are rejected — regular certified cleaning keeps you covered on both counts.

  • Highly trained & certified staff
  • TR19 (DW/172) compliant extraction cleaning
  • £5 million public liability cover
  • Structured reporting provided
  • Flexible scheduling available
  • UK-wide coverage
  • Recurring contract options
  • 24/7 customer support
