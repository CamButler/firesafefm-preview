Everything you need to know before you book

Straightforward answers on TR19 compliance, cleaning frequency, coverage and how to get a quote.

TR19 (also referenced as DW/172) is the UK industry standard covering the cleanliness of kitchen extraction systems. It sets out how ductwork, canopies and filters should be inspected and cleaned to reduce fire risk. Most commercial insurance policies require evidence of TR19-compliant cleaning, and a build-up of grease in an uncleaned system is one of the most common causes of commercial kitchen fires.

TR19 recommends a frequency based on usage: typically every 3 months for heavy use (e.g. fast food, 12+ hours a day), every 6 months for moderate use, and annually for light or seasonal use. We'll assess your kitchen on-site and recommend the right schedule for you.
